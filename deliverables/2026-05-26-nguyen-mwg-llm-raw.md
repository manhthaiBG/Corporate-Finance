**Primary driver — Asset Turnover (2.21×):** For a retail group operating on thin margins, volume throughput is the dominant value lever. MWG's ability to generate VND 155,928 billion in revenue from a VND 70,437 billion starting asset base reflects aggressive inventory velocity and capital efficiency.

**Debt Burden (0.857×):** Below 1.0× because Non-Operating Financial Income (VND 3,107 billion) supplements EBIT-level earnings in absolute terms, but the ratio reflects that net income is lower than ATOI as a percentage — an unusual structure driven by structural reliance on treasury carry income.

**ALM Interest Rate Gap Stress-Test:**  
MWG carries VND 29,931 billion in short-term floating-rate borrowings against VND 38,874 billion in cash & financial investments (of which ~VND 33,874 billion is in fixed-term deposits). In Q1/2026's tightening cycle, borrowing rates reprice immediately upon rollover while deposit yields remain locked.

A 100 bps shock on VND 29,931 billion → additional annual interest of ~VND 299 billion:  
- TIE compresses: VND 7,075B / VND 1,770B → **~4.00×** *(from 4.81×)*
- Net Margin erodes by ~0.19 pp (VND 299B / VND 155,928B)
- Deposit-side carry spread narrows: if fixed deposits yield 5.5% and new borrowing costs rise to 6.5%, the ~VND 3,107B financial income buffer begins to compress

**Assessment:** The ALM structure is a strategic carry trade that has been profitable in the FY2024–2025 easing cycle. In Q1/2026 tightening, it becomes an unhedged refinancing risk. The Board must decide whether to lock in current rates before Q3/2026 rollovers.

**BHX Expansion Sensitivity:** A 10% asset base expansion (BHX store rollout) with flat FY2025 revenue would compress Asset Turnover from 2.21× to ~2.01×, reducing Du Pont ROE by approximately 2 percentage points — a meaningful headwind if BHX Northern Vietnam ramp-up lags targets.

## Strategic Recommendations

### R1: Initiate an Interest Rate Swap Programme to Fix 50% of Short-Term Floating Debt

- **Ratio evidence:** TIE = 4.81×; ALM stress-test: 100 bps shock → TIE ~4.00×; ST Borrowings = VND 29,931B
- **Observation:** All VND 29,931 billion in short-term borrowings reprices immediately upon rollover, creating unhedged exposure to Q1/2026 rate tightening that could compress TIE to 4.00× or below if rates rise 150+ bps.
- **Recommendation:** Instruct the Group CFO to negotiate a fixed-rate interest rate swap covering 50% of the VND 29,931 billion short-term borrowing facility (approximately VND 15,000 billion) before Q3/2026 rollover dates, locking in current rates and capping TIE downside at 4.0× under a 150 bps shock scenario.
- **Risk if not acted upon:** TIE falls below 3.5×, elevating refinancing risk perception and potentially triggering covenant review by key lending banks, at a time when BHX Northern rollout demands continued working capital support.

### R2: Structure BHX Northern Expansion via Segment-Level Equity Raise, Not Group Borrowing

- **Ratio evidence:** Total Debt Ratio = 60.5%; Asset Turnover = 2.21×; Du Pont sensitivity: 10% asset expansion → AT 2.01× → ~2pp ROE compression
- **Observation:** Funding BHX Northern rollout through additional group-level short-term debt would push Total Debt Ratio above 65% and compress asset turnover below 2.0×, degrading ROE to approximately 23%.
- **Recommendation:** Authorize the BHX segment CFO to commence a standalone minority equity raise (targeting VND 5,000–8,000 billion from strategic grocery-sector investors) to fund the Northern store rollout over FY2026–2027, ring-fencing expansion capital from MWG's consolidated balance sheet and preserving group Total Debt Ratio below 60%.
- **Risk if not acted upon:** Group leverage exceeds 65% Total Debt Ratio, constraining working capital headroom for the ICT segment during seasonal peak periods (Q3–Q4 smartphone launches).

### R3: Board Resolution to Disclose Treasury Carry Income Separately and Cap Financial Income Dependency

- **Ratio evidence:** Operating Profit Margin = 5.29%; Net Profit Margin = 4.54% (counter-intuitive 0.75 pp gap explained by financial income); Financial Income = VND 3,107B (~36% of EBT)
- **Observation:** Treasury carry income (borrowing short at ~5%, placing at ~6–7% in fixed deposits) has propped net income by approximately VND 3,107 billion in FY2025. This income is rate-sensitive and non-recurring in a tightening cycle — yet it is consolidated into reported net margin without segment-level transparency.
- **Recommendation:** Issue a Board resolution requiring (a) separate disclosure of financial income in quarterly earnings presentations with an explicit earnings quality caveat; (b) a target ceiling of VND 2,000 billion in annual financial income contribution (reducing dependency from 36% to ~22% of EBT by FY2027); (c) accelerate retail-side EBIT improvement to offset the carry-income drawdown through renegotiation of vendor payment terms and SG&A reduction.
- **Risk if not acted upon:** If carry spread compresses to zero (deposit yield = borrowing rate), Net Margin falls from 4.54% to approximately 2.9%, erasing approximately 39% of FY2025 reported profitability with no forewarning to investors.

### R4: Approve BHX Segment Financial Reporting Before IPO Filing to Close the Valuation Discount

- **Ratio evidence:** MVA = VND 96,745.5B; M/B = 3.91×; BHX standalone IPO valuation estimate ~VND 80,000B (~61% of market cap)
- **Observation:** MWG's blended M/B of 3.91× embeds a structural discount because the market cannot independently value BHX, An Khang, and the ICT segments without segment-level financial statements. Winmart+ (nearest listed grocery peer) trades at ~4.2× book — suggesting BHX's grocery operations alone may justify a higher multiple than the consolidated entity currently receives.
- **Recommendation:** Authorize management to file segment financial statements (BHX grocery, ICT retail, pharmacy) with HOSE within two reporting cycles, adopting a three-segment disclosure format that allows institutional investors to independently model BHX's VND 46,900 billion revenue base and ~VND 1,200 billion FY2026 net profit target. This disclosure is a prerequisite for a credible IPO valuation process.
- **Risk if not acted upon:** Without segment transparency, the BHX IPO will be priced at a discount to fair value, reducing MWG shareholder proceeds and potentially allowing Winmart+ to claim the "premium grocery platform" narrative with institutional investors ahead of the listing.
